  1. “The generation that is protesting has lived its whole life in one crisis after another”

    Vroči mikrofon (“Hot Mic”), Val 202, RTV SlovenijaRadio·19 November 2026·47 min·In SlovenianInterviewer: Gašper Andrinek

    A conversation on the state of democracy in Europe. Democracy is not a weak model, but a fragile one, and we have treated it for too long as if it would survive whatever we did to it. Why the young people protesting across Europe are right to be angry, what the students’ victory in Serbia means for the EU, why security for democracy is not enough without security through democracy, what Putin is really after, and why the fate of the UN and the Council of Europe is not someone else’s business.
